About

I like experiments that end with a clear answer, even when the answer is no.

I studied Biochemistry at the Universidad de Castilla-La Mancha, graduating with honours in cell biology, and moved to Vienna in 2024 for the MSc in Biotechnology at BOKU, where I defended my thesis on non-canonical autophagy in Arabidopsis thaliana.

From January 2025 to June 2026 I was a research assistant in Yasin Dagdas’s group at the Gregor Mendel Institute (Vienna BioCenter), working with postdoc Jose Julián Valenzuela. When Dagdas relocated to Heidelberg, I helped carry the ongoing work into the newly independent Groß/Valenzuela lab at BOKU.

Before research I worked in food-safety quality control at Campofrío in Burgos, Spain: sample management, microbiological testing and the discipline of a routine lab. Outside the lab I enjoy design, 3D modelling and building small tools for myself.

Selected projects

AlphaFold model

MSc thesis · GMI → BOKU · 2025–2026

Decoding ATG8ylation under cell wall damage

When the cell wall fails, the vacuole risks collapsing. Plants protect it by attaching ATG8 directly to the vacuolar membrane, a non-canonical form of autophagy. My MSc thesis asked what switches this on: I characterised candidate regulators in transgenic Arabidopsis lines by confocal and electron microscopy. Results are unpublished, so details are available on request.

Along the way I covered the whole loop at the bench, from building constructs to imaging root tips. Funded by the Austrian Science Fund (FWF) and carried out at GMI and BOKU.

  1. CloneGreenGate, SnapGene
  2. VerifyDNA extraction, sequencing
  3. TransformE. coli → Agrobacterium
  4. Floral dipTransgenic lines
  5. ImageConfocal, root tips
  6. MeasureHuygens, FIJI

Cellulose synthase trimer · PDB 6WLB

MSc side project · BOKU · 2026

From docking scores to root assays

Structure-based virtual screening of analogues of the cellulose synthase inhibitor ES20-1 against the CESA6 catalytic domain, using cryo-EM, crystal and AlphaFold references cross-validated with FATCAT. I then took the top predictions to the bench and tested them in an Arabidopsis Col-0 root-growth inhibition assay.

  • AutoDock Vina
  • AlphaFold
  • FATCAT
  • ChimeraX
  • Root-growth assay

Bachelor’s thesis on modeling the chemokine receptor CCR5, the co-receptor HIV uses to enter immune cells, and docking ligands against it as a therapeutic target. My first step into structural biology.

Food-safety quality control

Three months in a routine microbiology and physicochemistry lab of a large meat producer. Meat, surface and water samples were logged in iLab and taken through Salmonella detection (enrichment in Fraser broth, then SX2 tubes at 42 °C), automated TEMPO microbial counts, culture-media preparation, and rapid gluten and phosphate tests whose result decides what goes on the product label.
